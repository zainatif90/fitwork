Freelancers lose hours on listings never meant for them. FitWork reads every job for you, understands what it means — not just its keywords — and shows the 5 jobs that truly fit you, each with a score you can trust and a reason you can read.

Inside the model · meaning-space
Every job and every profile becomes a point in a 384-dimensional space of meaning. Similar work lands close together — even when it uses different words. Your nearest points are your best matches.

How it works · 5 steps, repeated every 30 minutes
STEP 1

Collect

Real jobs from Freelancer.com and 3 job boards.

STEP 2

Understand

MiniLM turns text into 384 numbers of meaning.

STEP 3

Search

pgvector finds the 50 nearest jobs in milliseconds.

STEP 4

Score

A 0–100 Match Score, with the reason.

STEP 5

Act

AI drafts the proposal or picks the best teammate.
